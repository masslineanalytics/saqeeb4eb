Our Four Priorities

A focused student-first agenda built around voice, transparency, respected staff, and belonging.

Our Four Priorities

  • Empowered Students
  • Clear Dashboards
  • Better Paid Staff
  • Safe & Inclusive Schools

East Brunswick schools should put students first by listening to them, giving families clear information, investing in staff, and ensuring every child feels safe and included.

Students First

Empowered Students

Every student deserves the opportunity to learn, grow, and have a meaningful voice in their education.

  • Create student committees that can develop proposals and present them to the Board.
  • Expand fair access to advanced coursework by reviewing placement barriers and unnecessary testing.
  • Strengthen special education services, student support, and communication with families.
  • Work toward ending student lunch debt so no child is punished for needing a meal.

Students First

Clear Dashboards

Families deserve clear, accessible information about how decisions are made and how public money is spent.

  • Publish easy-to-understand dashboards for budgets, staffing, programs, construction, and district outcomes.
  • Report progress on Board goals and major initiatives.
  • Explain policy changes, program reductions, contracts, and major spending decisions in plain language.

Students First

Better Paid Staff

Strong schools begin with supporting the people who serve students every day.

  • Support competitive pay and benefits for educators and school staff.
  • Improve recruitment, retention, and working conditions.
  • Expand access to counselors, case managers, aides, and other student-support professionals.

Students First

Safe & Inclusive Schools

Every student deserves to feel safe, respected, and welcomed in East Brunswick schools.

  • Improve education, reporting, communication, and fair investigations around hate incidents.
  • Protect students from Islamophobia, antisemitism, racism, and other forms of discrimination.
  • Celebrate East Brunswick's cultures, languages, and communities through stronger family and community partnerships.
  • Treat parents as partners and make district communication accessible to every family.
