About Muhammad

Muhammad Saqeeb is a community organizer committed to bringing students, parents, and educators into the decisions that shape their schools.

Muhammad has organized youth service projects and volunteered with Urdu and Hindi language schools serving students in grades K–8. He believes strong communities are built by investing in young people and creating opportunities for families to participate in public life.

Having attended East Brunswick Public Schools from third grade through high school as a special education student, Muhammad understands firsthand the importance of ensuring every child has the opportunity to succeed.

Through years of independent study and graduate education, he developed a passion for using data, research, and evidence to improve decision-making, strategic planning, and public policy.

Today, Muhammad is running for the East Brunswick Board of Education to help build schools where every student has the freedom to learn, grow, and succeed in a way that reflects their individual strengths.
